# Sol low for visible computer use

The experimental `computer-use` profile starts at GPT-6 Sol low through Codex
and Pi's `openai-codex` provider. Medium, high, and xhigh remain explicit
comparison tiers. The route does not escalate after an uncertain or failed
desktop action; the caller interprets the result and chooses any later tier.

This is an operator-directed trial of Sol's effort curve on bounded, visible
desktop tasks. The vendor reports computer-use capability for the model, but
the available result does not isolate low effort or measure this desktop tool.
The route stays experimental until task outcomes support a stable seat.

Sol max is prohibited across routing selections. No measured gain establishes
a reason to spend that tier on the trial, while low through xhigh remain
available for comparison. The prohibition is enforced by the roster and by
`agent-routing check` and `validate`.
