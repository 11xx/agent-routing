# GPT-6 Luna max on two bounded muzaiten lanes (2026-10-07)

`gpt-6-luna#max` ran through Codex on two different, lead-briefed muzaiten
lanes. These observations extend its bounded implementation record; they are
not paired comparisons with another model. Neither Codex session reported a
provider-dollar field or subscription spend. The two first-output observations
come from different session/supervisor paths and are not a latency comparison.

| Lane | Wall time including tools | First observed output | Session token counts | Result |
| :-- | --: | --: | :-- | :-- |
| Warm CLI cache-stream memory | 32m36s | 8.90s | 10,620,107 input, including 10,449,152 cached; 94,014 output, including 70,968 reasoning | Integrated signed `6e40c65` after lead repairs |
| Isolated artwork-cache measurement | 13m26s | 6.20s | 2,798,914 input, including 2,652,160 cached; 61,869 output, including 43,537 reasoning | Measured and archived; no product policy or source edit |

The cache lane repaired an actual caller-visible memory shape: ephemeral
metadata decoding for warm CLI streams left retained GUI indexes interning.
Twelve paired synthetic reader trials matched complete record digests. At
100k high-cardinality rows median peak RSS fell from 120,828 to 20,068 KiB
(83.4%). The delegate's private benchmark harness needed two compile/link
repairs. The lead corrected documentation and made an optional PTY test skip
when its utility is absent. Exact-head build and 82/82 tests passed; the
signed change was integrated locally with independent-review debt. The
benchmark is a synthetic reader boundary, not a whole-app memory claim.

The artwork lane ran a real cache snapshot against six synthetic source
revisions at three sizes. Its 1x/10x results accumulated 288/2,880 rows and
66,392,829/680,738,253 PNG bytes. Delete-only SQL prototypes freed pages but
did not shrink an SQLite main file; pinned readers permitted much larger WAL
files. Every current cover matched exact pixels after maintenance prototypes.
The lead independently checked 1,312 evidence hashes and queried the six
SQLite files read-only. An initial manifest-relative-path error was repaired;
all workload stages passed without compiler diagnostics. No product cap,
worker maintenance contract or migration was selected.

The first lane establishes useful bounded execution with lead repair and
integration; the second establishes careful measurement and an honest stop at
a policy boundary. Different tasks and build loads prevent deriving a model
speed or quality ranking from their wall times. No independent review was
claimed for the cache patch, and the artwork change remains open.
