# DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017

DeepSeek released V4.1 Flash 1017 on 2026-10-17. The canonical roster ID
`deepseek-flash`, direct API ID `deepseek-flash`, and OpenCode Go ID
`deepseek-v4.1-flash` remain stable. The roster records the checkpoint as
`build = "DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017"`. Hosted aliases are rolling IDs; this
records the expected checkpoint, not an attestation by the Go endpoint.

The operator retains every existing seat and its max default. Low and high
are available for explicit selection. The installed Pi catalog maps low,
high and max directly for both DeepSeek and OpenCode Go. No medium or xhigh
tier is inferred from Pi's generic CLI options. No local comparison measures
the 1017 quality, reasoning-volume or latency curve; max is a preference,
not a measured optimum.

Every field arc, benchmark, latency measurement and review observation must
name its checkpoint, provider, harness and requested effort. Provider-reported
identity or its absence must be recorded separately. Evidence through
2026-10-02 below belongs to September V4.1 Flash; the 53% / $0.46 board row
belongs to V4 Flash 0731. Neither measures 1017. New observations belong to
1017 while that is the roster's current build and must retain that label
when the rolling alias advances.

Sources checked 2026-10-18:

- https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news261017/
- https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/

  low/high/max and disable medium/xhigh. Catalog mapping is transport evidence,
  not model-quality evidence or confirmation of the hosted checkpoint.
