# 2026-09-03 — The September releases enter, and no seat moves

Seven models entered or changed on the roster in one pass, and the routing
table is the same afterwards. That is the point of the pass: the roster's
rule is that a seat moves on a field arc, and a release week produces
measurements, not arcs.

2026-09-02, and it now measures several rows this roster carried only as
vendor or third-party claims. Each moved into the pass@1 column at the
effort the board ran:

  its cash, on 166 steps and 143k output tokens per task. The top of the

  Flash tier is already routed. Six points over Flash for seventeen times the
  cash, which is Sol-over-Luna's shape on a pool that is not already paid for.

  nine times Luna's cash.
- DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813, max: 63% for $1.67. The April preview measured 8%;
  the generally available build measures 55 points higher on the same board.
  The row's status moved from restricted to experimental and its role did
  not: the fabrication record is the family's, and one independent row on a
  build with no field record here retires a number, not a posture.
- Muse Spark 1.2, xhigh: 55% for $3.70, exactly the Opus-4.8 band the
  third-party index predicted. The probationary review seat was earned by a

## What entered unmeasured

in Claude Code and through the first-party backend — which is the fact the
Fable 5 retirement turned on. It enters as an experimental row with an
explicit-only high tier and no profile. The promotion bar is stated on the
row: a Datacurve row, an observed quota ratio against Opus 5 on this plan,
then one field arc under a lead role. Two facts argue against reseating on
launch evidence even after that: it is metered like Fable 5, which weighed
roughly twice an Opus session, and it is capped at half of a Max plan's
weekly allowance, so it cannot be the only head model whatever it scores.
A person may select it interactively; the harness records that, and the
roster does not make the choice for them.

prices as 1.2 and tops an aggregator board at 75.4% — under that board's
harness, which scores Opus 5 at 68.8% where this matrix measures 74%, so
the figure is recorded as third-party and nothing else. The review seat
stays on 1.2 until 1.3 is measured on the review arc 1.2 was; a successor
inherits a predecessor's weaknesses, not its record.

Astra is announced, unreleased, and unpriced. It has a row so its arrival
is a data update rather than a policy rewrite, and it enters, when it
enters, through the Sol seat's evidence bar.
