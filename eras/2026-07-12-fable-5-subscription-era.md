## Fable's role when accessible

- **Peer lead for taste and intent** — inferred unsaid intent behind
  prompts, UX/copy quality, public API ergonomics, architectural taste,
  final user-facing review. On these axes it beat every GPT-5.6 tier and
  every other Claude model.
- **Orchestrator** — first-rate at decomposing work for subagents and
  synthesizing multi-harness results; the proven arc shape late in the
  era was Fable orchestrating with gpt-5.6-terra as the implementation
  workhorse (near-zero corrections, cheaper on all counts).
- **Landing-quality implementer and simplifier** — tends toward the smaller
  diff that can merge, especially for ambiguous product work, architecture,
  and novel UI. It is also effective at simplifying another model's output.
- deepswe 2026-07-10 anchors: fable-5 high 69% / $9.18 / 57k out-tokens /
  59 steps; xhigh 70% / $13.41; max 70% / $21.63.

## Rules the era established (still true if it returns)

- **Never run Fable above high.** xhigh/max add ≤1pt for 1.5–2.4× cost
  and overthink per step. (The same non-scaling pattern later capped sol
  at xhigh.)
- Parity ladder vs GPT-5.6: sol medium ≈ fable low; sol high ≈ fable
  high; sol xhigh ≈ fable xhigh. Sol matched Fable on technical
  intelligence at ~38% of the cost; Fable kept winning only when the
  bottleneck was unsaid intent or taste.
- Fable quota was the scarce budget: spend it on orchestration, judgment,
  and taste turns; never on labor a delegate could do.
- List non-negotiable details literally: Fable may pursue inferred intent and
  lose a constraint. Do not use Fable as the independent judge of its own
  output; retain Sol or another independent reviewer.

## How the seat was filled afterwards

- 2026-07-12 → 2026-07-25: opus-4.8 high held the taste/intent axis, at a
  real capability drop (52% vs 69% on deepswe, weaker intent inference),
  compensated with more explicit briefs and heavier user review.
- From 2026-07-25: opus-5 high, as head model rather than a fallback. It
  matches Fable's practical capability more efficiently at roughly half the
  subscription quota burn, without Fable's ceiling on the hardest
  reasoning — see `2026-07-25-opus-5-head-model-era.md`.
- Implementation workhorse under an orchestrator from 2026-07-18 through the
  2026-07-30 price reset: gpt-5.6-sol low. Terra high then took the default
  bounded-work role; Luna high entered probation for pre-resolved trials.

## Selection rule

None. Fable is retired: never route to it, never offer it as an escalation,
and do not treat residual credit-billed access as availability.
